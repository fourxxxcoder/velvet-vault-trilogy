**The Velvet Trilogy** — three heists, one night. Wren the thief hits the Meridian Gallery, the Aurum Bank and the Velvet Casino: 3 heists, 15 guards, 15 cameras, 30 loot pieces, 3 laser grids, 2 keycard doors, and a safe to crack in every room.

- Guard vision cones you have to read before you move; suspicion that hunts, chases that catch, and a fair caught moment that costs a life, not the night.
- Cameras that feed the alarm meter; a full meter means LOCKDOWN, and every guard hunts you.
- Lasers on a blink cycle, keycard doors, and a safe-cracking minigame: hold SPACE, release the needle inside the gold band, three tumblers to open the vault.
- Four tools at a cost: coin lures, smoke pellets, and an EMP that kills the cameras — loudly.
- Three routes through every room, a getaway that pays for speed, ghost play and spare tools, and an S/A/B/C grade per heist.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best heist game" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD/arrows to move, Shift to sneak, click (or Q) to throw a lure, E for smoke, F for EMP, hold SPACE at a vault to crack it, P to pause, 1/2/3 to pick a heist.
